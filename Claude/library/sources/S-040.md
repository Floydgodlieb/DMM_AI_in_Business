  [p. 1, Abstract] We conducted an empirical study encompassing 12,108 SMEs, based on survey data of the Flash Eurobarometer database from the European Union.
  [p. 1, Abstract] Contrary to prevailing literature, business environmental support alone demonstrates limited impact, emphasizing its contingent effectiveness within a well-elaborated institutional framework.
  [p. 1, Abstract] However, internal capabilities exert a greater influence on AI adoption in SMEs compared to business environmental support.
  [p. 1, Introduction] These challenges include financial constraints [10], a lack of expertise and skills [8,11], resistance to change, and difficulties in integrating AI with existing systems [12].
  [p. 6, 4.1] This survey, conducted between February and May 2020, explores diverse topics, including innovation and digital technologies.
  [p. 6, 4.3] Regarding size, we categorized the companies into three groups: from 0 to 9 employees as micro-enterprises; from 10 to 49 employees as small enterprises; and from 50 to 250 employees as medium-sized enterprises.
  [p. 6, Table 1 and Table 2] Sample size distribution: 1 to 9 employees 7708 (63.7 %); 10 to 49 employees 2571 (21.2 %); 50 to 250 employees 1829 (15.1 %); total 12108. Sample geographical distribution includes NL - The Netherlands 474 (3.9 %).
  [p. 8, 5] It is evident that digitalisation capabilities have the most significant impact on AI (digitalisation: .730; 100 % normalized value), followed by innovation capabilities (innovation: .195; 26.7 % normalized value), with business environmental support having the least impact on the probability of adopting AI (digitalisation: .075; 10.3 % normalized value).
  [p. 9, 5] Moreover, when we examine node 5 combined with node 15, we observe a synergistic effect between the variables of digital capabilities and innovation capabilities.
  [p. 12, 7 Conclusion] Second, our research challenges the traditionally recognized role of business environmental support.
