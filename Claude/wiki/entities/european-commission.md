# European Commission

The EU's executive. In the library it is the source for the duties a trader faces and for the hub network that is meant to help SMEs.

## What the library has from the Commission

- **Sanctions:** the Russia export bans and their legal basis [W2-006] [W2-008]. See [[sanctions-and-export-screening]].
- **Customs:** the adopted customs reform and the EU Customs Data Hub [W4-079] [W4-081]. See [[ai-in-trade-and-customs]].
- **GDPR:** standard contractual clauses for processors [W2-020]. See [[data-location-and-gdpr]].
- **AI Act:** the AI-literacy Q&A [W4-003] [W4-006] and, from the AI Act Service Desk, the texts of Article 4 [W4-091], Article 26 [W4-093] and Article 99 [W4-096]. See [[ai-literacy-duty]], [[ai-act]], [[ai-risk-management]].
- **Competitiveness:** the Draghi report [W4-001]. See [[geopolitics-of-ai]].
- **Hubs:** the EDIH catalogue and the JRC's results [W4-033]. See [[edih]].
- **Statistics:** Eurostat's adoption figures [W4-118]. See [[eurostat]].
