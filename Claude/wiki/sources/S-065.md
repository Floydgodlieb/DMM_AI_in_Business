# S-065 · Determinants of AI adoption intention in SMEs. Romanian case study (Apostoaie, Roman, Maxim, Jijie; Journal of Business Economics and Management 26(2), 277-296, 2025; DOI 10.3846/jbem.2025.23650; CC BY 4.0)

## What it says

- A peer-reviewed study investigates what drives and what hinders AI adoption in Romanian SMEs; it is an exploratory study of Romania, not the Netherlands [W4-148].
- It uses a survey of 145 SME respondents in four Romanian sectors; respondents report on themselves and the sample is small [W4-147].
- It quotes an EIB figure, not its own data: in the EU only 30% of microenterprises reported taking steps to enhance digitalization in 2022, against 62% of large firms [W4-149].
