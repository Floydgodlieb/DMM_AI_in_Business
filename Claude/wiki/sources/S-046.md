# S-046 · Detailed Report on The Adoption of Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning in Customs (Smart Customs Project, March 2025)

## What it says

- Conflict of interest and status: the report was funded by China Customs and, by its own notice, has not been through the formal WCO approval process [W4-083].
- It says AI and machine learning let customs administrations automate routine processes, improve risk assessment and fraud detection, use resources better and speed up clearance; it describes customs authorities, not traders [W4-084].
- It recommends a thorough cost-benefit analysis to justify AI investments and pilot projects before full roll-out [W4-085].
- It says data availability, quality and management are fundamental to the success of AI projects and need significant investment in data preparation and governance [W4-086].
