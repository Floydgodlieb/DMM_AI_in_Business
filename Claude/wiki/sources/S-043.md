# S-043 · Does Machine Translation Affect International Trade? Evidence from a Large Digital Platform

## What it says

- An NBER working paper finds that introducing machine translation on the eBay platform significantly increased international trade there, raising exports by 17.5% [W4-071].
- The authors call this causal evidence that language barriers significantly hinder trade, and that AI had already begun to improve economic efficiency in at least one area [W4-072].
- The platform is large: more than 14 billion dollars of trade among more than 200 countries in 2014 [W4-073]; but it is a consumer and small-seller marketplace, not B2B used machinery [W4-071].
- It is a working paper dated August 2018, so it is evidence from before generative AI [W4-074].
