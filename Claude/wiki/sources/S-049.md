# S-049 · Article 26: Obligations of deployers of high-risk AI systems (Regulation (EU) 2024/1689), AI Act Service Desk

## What it says

- Article 26: a business that uses a high-risk AI system must take appropriate technical and organisational measures to use it according to the supplier's instructions; this applies only to systems that count as high-risk [W4-093].
- It must assign human oversight to people with the necessary competence, training, authority and support [W4-094].
- It must keep the logs the system generates automatically, as far as they are under its control, for a period suited to the purpose and at least six months [W4-095].
- This is the primary text, replacing the mirror ([[S-005]]) as the better source for these duties.
