# S-066 · Barriers to the implementation of artificial intelligence in small and medium-sized enterprises: Pilot study (Zavodna, Überwimmer, Frankus; Journal of Economics and Management 46(1), 331-352, 2024; DOI 10.22367/jem.2024.46.13; CC BY-NC 4.0)

## What it says

- A peer-reviewed pilot study identifies four main impediments to AI implementation in SMEs, from 22 interviews with industry experts in the Czech Republic and Austria [W4-150].
- The first is a notable lack of trust [W4-151]; the second a knowledge gap that hampers progress [W4-152].
- The third is infrastructure limitations: inadequate computing resources, outdated systems or insufficient technical support [W4-153].
- The authors note it is qualitative research with a smaller sample, so findings are indicative, not representative [W4-154].
