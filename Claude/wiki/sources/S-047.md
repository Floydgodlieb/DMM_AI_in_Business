# S-047 · Generative AI and Sales Productivity: Field Experiments in Online Retail

## What it says

- Label: a preprint, not peer-reviewed; some authors consulted for the partner company and one was employed by it [W4-087].
- Field experiments on one cross-border e-commerce platform found generative AI increased sales in most workflows, with effects from no detectable impact up to 16.3% [W4-087].
- The sales gains did not come with worse return rates or customer ratings [W4-088].
- The four applications with positive effects are worth roughly 5 dollars per consumer per year to a large consumer retailer [W4-089].
- The platform deployed generative AI in seven workflows between September 2023 and June 2024, including a pre-sale chatbot and live chat translation [W4-090].
- The setting is consumer-facing sellers, not business-to-business machinery [W4-087].
