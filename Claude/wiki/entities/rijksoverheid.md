# Rijksoverheid (Dutch central government)

Three government channels appear in the library. Two are written for government bodies, so their advice applies to a trader only by analogy.

## What the library has

- **Algoritmekader** (for government bodies): staff must be sufficiently AI-literate [W4-008]; ask the supplier what training is needed [W4-010].
- **Ondernemersplein** (for entrepreneurs): the AI Act binds users as well as providers [W4-011].
- **Digitale Overheid** (reporting the Dutch DPA): management must agenda and budget AI literacy [W4-038].

See [[ai-literacy-duty]] and [[ai-act]].
