# S-052 · A Survey on Hallucination in Large Language Models: Principles, Taxonomy, Challenges, and Open Questions (Huang et al.; ACM Transactions on Information Systems, 2025)

## What it says

- A peer-reviewed survey says large language models are prone to hallucination: they generate content that sounds plausible but is not factual [W4-101].
- Because the information can directly influence decisions, misleading output can spread false beliefs or even cause harm [W4-102].
- Even systems that look answers up in documents first (retrieval-augmented generation) have limitations and still suffer from hallucinations [W4-103].
