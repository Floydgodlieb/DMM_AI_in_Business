# S-071 · Generative AI and the SME Workforce: New Survey Evidence

## What it says

- The OECD survey covered 5 232 SMEs in Austria, Canada, Germany, Ireland, Japan, Korea and the United Kingdom, run by Ipsos; the Netherlands is not included and SMEs report on themselves [W4-171].
- SMEs are defined as companies with up to 249 employees, including one-person companies [W4-172].
- Generative AI is used in 31% of SMEs, from 24% in Japan to 39% in Germany [W4-173].
- Of SMEs using it, 65% say it helped increase employee performance, against 35% for scaling up, 29% for competing with larger firms and 26% for revenue [W4-174]; 83% report no effect on overall staff need [W4-175].
- Barriers: unsuitability to the SME's work (57% of non-adopters), copyright, legal or regulatory worries (54%), worry about what happens to information fed into the models (52%) and lack of staff skills (50%) [W4-176].
- A third or fewer of SMEs using generative AI train staff, set internal guidelines or research legal issues [W4-177].
