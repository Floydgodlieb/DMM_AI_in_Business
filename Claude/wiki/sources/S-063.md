# S-063 · AI in de samenleving: ervaringen en opinies, hoofdstuk 4 Opvattingen over AI op de arbeidsmarkt (CBS, 25-2-2026)

## What it says

- CBS: of people in paid work, a little over 2 in 5 (43 percent) use AI in their work; this is a survey of Dutch adults, not of firms [W4-139].
- Dutch adults mostly think AI will make certain jobs disappear (75 percent) and cause staff to lose knowledge and skills (64 percent); this is opinion, not measurement [W4-140].
- On the other hand, 57 percent think AI can raise productivity because work can be done faster [W4-141].
- A majority (70 percent) expect to use AI more often in their work over the next ten years [W4-142].
