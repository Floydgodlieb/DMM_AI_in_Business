excerpt: |
  (Article 14(1)) "High-risk AI systems shall be designed and developed in such a way,
  including with appropriate human-machine interface tools, that they can be effectively
  overseen by natural persons during the period in which they are in use."

  (Article 14(4)(d), override) deployer personnel must be able to "decide, in any particular
  situation, not to use the high-risk AI system or to otherwise disregard, override or reverse
  the output"

  (Article 14(4)(e), halt) personnel must be able to "intervene in the operation of the
  high-risk AI system or interrupt the system through a 'stop' button or a similar procedure
  that allows the system to come to a halt in a safe state"

  note: this article applies only to AI systems classified "high-risk" under Annex III (S-024
  confirms the compliance date for that classification was pushed back). A sales-chat or
  translation tool used by a trader in this sector is very unlikely to be on the Annex III list
  (biometrics, critical infrastructure, education, employment, essential services, law
  enforcement, migration, justice) — so this is presented in the draft as good practice to
  copy, not as a binding duty this reader currently faces.
