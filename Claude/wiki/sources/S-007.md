# S-007 · "AFM en DNB waarschuwen voor systeemrisico's financiële sector door digitale afhankelijkheid"

## What it says

- The Dutch financial sector faces growing risk from depending on a small number of non-European IT suppliers [W2-013].
- Because institutions rely on the same providers, concentration and systemic risks have emerged [W2-014].
- It is about the financial sector, not the vehicle trade: a mechanism, not a sector figure.
