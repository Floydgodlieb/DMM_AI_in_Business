# S-062 · Artificial Intelligence and the Labor Market (Hampole, Papanikolaou, Schmidt and Seegmiller; NBER Working Paper 33509, February 2025, revised September 2025)

## What it says

- An NBER working paper finds strong substitution of AI for tasks but modest overall employment effects, because reduced demand in exposed occupations is offset by higher labor demand at firms that adopt AI [W4-136].
- Firms that adopt AI see higher growth in revenue, measured productivity, profits and employment [W4-137].
- The authors conclude the overall impact of AI on the make-up of the workforce is limited because opposing forces offset each other [W4-138].
