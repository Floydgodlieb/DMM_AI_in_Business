  (EU Data Boundary, routing) "Microsoft Copilot calls to the LLM are routed to the
  closest data centers in the region, but also can call into other regions where
  capacity is available during high utilization periods."

  (EU Data Boundary scope) "For European Union (EU) users, we have additional
  safeguards to comply with the EU Data Boundary. EU traffic stays within the EU Data
  Boundary while worldwide traffic can be sent to the EU and other countries or regions
  for LLM processing."

  (subprocessor exclusion) "Models provided by Anthropic as a subprocessor are currently
  excluded from the EU Data Boundary."

  (data residency commitment) "Microsoft Copilot is upholding data residency
  commitments as outlined in the Microsoft Product Terms and Data Protection Addendum.
  Microsoft Copilot was added as a covered workload in the data residency commitments in
  Microsoft Product Terms on March 1, 2024."

  (regulatory commitment) "Microsoft is committed to complying with all laws and
  regulations applicable to Microsoft, including the EU AI Act, to enable our AI
  solutions to meet evolving standards for trustworthy and responsible AI."
