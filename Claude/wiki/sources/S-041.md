# S-041 · AI, robots and innovation in European SMEs

## What it says

- Firm traits such as size and internationalisation, and outside factors such as available digital skills, drive digitalisation [W4-054].
- European SMEs lag in advanced digital technologies because of complexity and cost [W4-055]; the largest firms adopt most [W4-061].
- Firms must build complementary skills, train staff and change their culture (citing Ciarli et al.) [W4-056], and invest in learning [W4-058].
- The needed skills are scarce and costly (citing Shapiro and Mandelman) [W4-057].
- Data: 16,365 firms, 19 February to 5 May 2020 [W4-059]; the Netherlands has no separate result [W4-060].
