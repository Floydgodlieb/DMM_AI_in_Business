# ECB (European Central Bank)

The euro area central bank. The library has a speech by a board member and a box in the Economic Bulletin built on its survey of firms.

## What the library has from the ECB

- Speech: employee AI adoption rose from 26 per cent in 2024 to 40 per cent in 2025 (a survey of consumers who are employees) [W4-111]; little evidence yet of a substantial employment effect [W4-112]; scenario gains of 0.3-0.4 points a year [W4-113]. See [[ai-and-jobs]], [[ai-and-the-economy]].
- Survey box (firm self-reports): 38% of euro area firms at an advanced stage of AI use [W4-114], with large and young firms ahead of small ones [W4-116]. See [[ai-adoption-rates]].
- Barriers among non-users: lack of usefulness first, then incompatibility and skills [W4-117]. See [[knowledge-gap]].
