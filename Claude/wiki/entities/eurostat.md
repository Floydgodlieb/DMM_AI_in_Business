# Eurostat

The statistical office of the European Union, part of the European Commission. Its figures are independently verifiable official statistics.

## What the library has from Eurostat

- In 2025, 20.0% of EU enterprises with 10+ staff used AI, up from 13.5% [W4-118]; highest in Denmark, lowest in Romania [W4-119]; the most common use was analysing written language [W4-120]. The survey covers firms with at least 10 employees or self-employed persons [W4-121]. See [[ai-adoption-rates]].
- Among EU firms that considered AI, lack of expertise was the main reason against it (70.89%) [W4-022]. See [[knowledge-gap]].
