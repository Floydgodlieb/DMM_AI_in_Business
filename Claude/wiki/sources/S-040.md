# S-040 · Analyzing AI adoption in European SMEs: A study of digital capabilities, innovation, and external environment

## What it says

- The study analyses 12,108 EU SMEs from the Flash Eurobarometer [W4-047], surveyed February to May 2020, before generative AI [W4-050].
- A firm's own capabilities matter more for AI adoption than outside support [W4-048]: innovation capabilities weigh 26.7% and outside support 10.3% of the digital-capability effect [W4-052].
- Digital and innovation capabilities reinforce each other [W4-053].
- Small firms face money limits, lack of expertise, resistance to change and integration problems [W4-049].
- Its small band is 10 to 49 staff, which covers most of the reader's range [W4-051].
