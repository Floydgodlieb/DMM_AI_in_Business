# KVK (Netherlands Chamber of Commerce)

Every Dutch firm is registered with KVK, which makes it the most familiar public channel for a small trader. It co-runs Ondernemersplein with the government.

## What the library has from KVK

- Data typed into an AI tool may not be protected [W4-020]; AI-made text may be wrong [W4-021]. See [[data-location-and-gdpr]] and [[ai-literacy-duty]].
- The AI Act's duties apply to users as well as providers [W4-011] (Ondernemersplein). See [[ai-act]].
- [[S-024]] (a KVK press release on entrepreneurs and the AI Act) has no claims yet.
