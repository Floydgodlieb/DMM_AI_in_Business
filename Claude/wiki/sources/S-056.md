# S-056 · Adopting and investing in AI: evidence from euro area firms in the SAFE (ECB Economic Bulletin, Issue 2/2026, box)

## What it says

- In the ECB's survey on the access to finance of enterprises, 38% of euro area firms are at an advanced stage of AI adoption, meaning significant or moderate use; firms report on themselves [W4-114].
- Around 45% of large firms and of listed or venture-capital-backed companies are at an advanced stage, rising to 56% for young firms [W4-115].
- Large firms, listed or venture-capital-backed companies and young firms adopt AI more often than small, unlisted and established firms [W4-116].
- Among firms not using AI, 30% name lack of usefulness as the main barrier, and around 20% name incompatibility with existing systems and a shortage of relevant skills [W4-117].
