excerpt: |
  (Article 3(4) text) "'deployer' means a natural or legal person, public authority, agency or
  other body using an AI system under its authority except where the AI system is used in the
  course of a personal non-professional activity"

  note: the definition does not require formal company approval or a procurement step — it
  turns on authority over the use and a professional (not personal) context. This is the basis
  for the claim that unauthorised staff use of AI still makes the business a "deployer."
