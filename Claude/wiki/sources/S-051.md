# S-051 · Artificial Intelligence Risk Management Framework (AI RMF 1.0), NIST AI 100-1

## What it says

- The US NIST framework is voluntary and meant to work for organisations of all sizes and sectors; it is a US framework, not EU law [W4-098].
- Its core has four functions: GOVERN, MAP, MEASURE and MANAGE [W4-099].
- GOVERN applies to all stages of an organisation's AI risk management, while MAP, MEASURE and MANAGE apply to specific systems and lifecycle stages [W4-100].
