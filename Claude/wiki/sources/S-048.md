# S-048 · Article 4: AI literacy (Regulation (EU) 2024/1689, AI Act), AI Act Service Desk

## What it says

- Article 4: companies that provide or use AI systems must take measures to build AI literacy among their staff and others who operate or use AI on their behalf, taking account of their knowledge, experience, training and the context of use [W4-091].
- The duty does not require a company to guarantee any specific level of AI literacy for any individual [W4-092].
