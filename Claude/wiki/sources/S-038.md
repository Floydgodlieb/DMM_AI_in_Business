# S-038 · AI-geletterdheid: AP roept organisaties op tot actie

## What it says

- Managers and directors must put AI literacy on the agenda, budget for it and actively support it [W4-038].
- The DPA asks organisations to organise AI literacy top-down as well as bottom-up [W4-039].
- Organisations that develop or use AI are legally required to make sure staff and contractors can use it responsibly [W4-040].
- This is a secondary report; the DPA's own document could not be fetched.
