# S-069 · Navigating the Jagged Technological Frontier: Field Experimental Evidence of the Effects of AI on Knowledge Worker Productivity and Quality (HBS Working Paper 24-013)

## What it says

- A Harvard Business School working paper, done with Boston Consulting Group (one author advises BCG), ran a pre-registered experiment with 758 consultants, about 7% of the firm's individual contributor-level consultants [W4-163].
- On 18 realistic tasks within the frontier of AI capabilities, consultants using AI completed 12.2% more tasks, 25.1% more quickly, with more than 40% higher quality than a control group [W4-164].
- On a task outside the frontier, consultants using AI were 19 percentage points less likely to produce correct solutions than those without AI [W4-165].
- The authors say professionals who navigate the frontier skillfully gain large benefits, while AI can decrease performance outside it [W4-166].
