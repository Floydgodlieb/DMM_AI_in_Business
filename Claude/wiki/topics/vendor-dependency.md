# Dependence on AI vendors

Firms that rely on the same few non-European providers share their risks; the library shows the mechanism in finance and the public sector, and one case that designs around it.

## The picture

- Dutch financial institutions depend increasingly on a few non-European IT suppliers [W2-013], which has created concentration and systemic risks [W2-014].
- Dutch public organisations depend on a handful of American tech firms for cloud, software and AI tools, which makes society vulnerable [W2-031].
- Outside the US and China, firms live in managed dependence on foreign AI providers [W2-019].
- A vendor's hosting can shift: Copilot calls can move to other regions under peak load [W2-028]. See [[data-location-and-gdpr]].

## The frontier case

- [[bas-world]] runs its agents on a vendor-agnostic foundation [W2-021], with models swappable plug-and-play [W2-022], built so it can continue without its implementation partner [W2-023]. This is company-reported and a frontier case, not the sector average.

## Gaps

- No claim measures vendor dependence among traders or SMEs.
