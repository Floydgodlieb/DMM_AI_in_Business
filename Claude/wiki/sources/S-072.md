# S-072 · AI adoption by small and medium-sized enterprises (OECD discussion paper for the G7)

## What it says

- AI use is consistently lower among SMEs than among large firms in all G7 countries [W4-178].
- Across the OECD, the share of large firms using AI (40%) is more than three times that of small firms (11.9%); this covers all AI, not only generative AI [W4-179].
- Between 2020 and 2024 the share of firms using AI rose from 5.6% to 14% across OECD member countries [W4-180].
- Skill shortages are a major barrier, consistently named by SMEs among their main hurdles [W4-181].
- SMEs face challenges and risks concerning accuracy, harmful content and legal uncertainty [W4-182].
