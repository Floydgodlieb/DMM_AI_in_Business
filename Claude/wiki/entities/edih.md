# European Digital Innovation Hubs (EDIH)

An EU network of regional hubs that help SMEs with digital transformation. The library has three Dutch hubs and the network's EU-wide results.

## Dutch hubs in the library

| Hub | Region | Fit for a used-vehicle trader |
| --- | --- | --- |
| EDIH Zuid-Nederland | Zeeland, Noord-Brabant, Limburg [W4-018] | Doubtful: aimed at manufacturing SMEs [W4-019] |
| Digital Hub Northwest Netherlands | North Holland, Utrecht, Flevoland [W4-012] | No: other regions, no trade focus [W4-012] |
| EDIH Northern Netherlands | Northern Netherlands | Unknown: [[S-023]] has no claims |

## Results, EU-wide

- Over 5 000 events and 18 000 services by September 2024 [W4-033].
- 90% of users improved digital maturity, by the network's own monitoring [W4-034]; AI and automation account for 35% of the increase [W4-035].
