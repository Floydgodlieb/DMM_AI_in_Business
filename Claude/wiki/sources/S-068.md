# S-068 · Strategic AI adoption in SMEs: A Prescriptive Framework (Hussain, Rizwan; arXiv:2408.11825, submitted 5 Aug 2024; preprint, not peer-reviewed)

## What it says

- Label: a preprint, not peer-reviewed, a conceptual framework with no data of its own [W4-160].
- It says SME AI adoption faces significant barriers, mainly cost, lack of technical skills and employee acceptance [W4-160].
- The framework starts with raising awareness and securing leadership commitment, then adopting low-cost, general-purpose AI tools to build skills and a positive attitude [W4-161].
- Its five phases are: awareness for leadership, general-purpose generative AI tools, off-the-shelf task-specific tools, in-house generative AI tools, in-house discriminative AI models [W4-162].
