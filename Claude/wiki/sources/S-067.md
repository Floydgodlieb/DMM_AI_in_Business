# S-067 · Bedrijven gebruiken AI vaakst voor marketing of verkoop (CBS nieuwsbericht, 12-12-2025, ICT-gebruik bij bedrijven 2025)

## What it says

- CBS: one in six Dutch businesses uses AI in 2025, double the share two years earlier; the 2025 figures are provisional [W4-155].
- The increase is strongest among businesses with 50 to 250 employees, from 20 percent in 2023 to 45 percent in 2025 [W4-156].
- Of the businesses that use AI, 35 percent do so for marketing or sales [W4-157].
- Three quarters of businesses do not use AI and have not considered it [W4-158].
- 49 percent of businesses name privacy as a reason not to use AI and 42 percent name legal consequences [W4-159].
