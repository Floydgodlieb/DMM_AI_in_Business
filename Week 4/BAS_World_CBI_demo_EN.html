Why this is an agentic tool

"Explain why this is an agentic tool: LLM, Skills, Tools, Context (PRD, research proposal, blueprint, build plan with memory)." Click a concept.

SayAn AI model works towards one goal over several steps: a checked page. It uses tools, follows skills and a fixed context, and keeps its memory in files. A failed check sends the work back. A person approves and publishes.

Deliberately bounded

  • The agent does: search, appraise, translate, write, check itself, and stop and ask when in doubt.
  • A person does: approve after every stage, fill in verified_by, process the interview, publish.
  • In weeks 2 and 3 the PM runs the steps. From week 4 an orchestrator takes over one step at a time, once that step passes its test.

From sub-question to interview question

"Explain how you translated your research question and sub questions into interview questions, and whether working on the interview guide was a reason to improve your research proposal." Click a sub-question.

SayThree sub-questions can only be answered inside the company. Three of us each wrote a guide; we tested all 30 questions on six points and built one new guide from them.

    How the 30 original questions were tested

    • Does it feed a sub-question or criterion?
    • Does it ask for an example or a number?
    • Is it neutral, not leading?
    • Can this person know the answer?
    • One thing at a time?
    • Does it help the handbook page?

    Did it improve the proposal?

    Honestly: the big changes from v1 to v2 came from the week-1 feedback. Working on the guides gave four points for v2.1:

    • Concrete examples for the boundary question
    • The budget holder as the source for I2
    • Name the chain steps explicitly
    • Name S-012 as a limitation

    Pushbacks and live option

    Open a question when the teacher asks it. Rule of thumb: admit a gap first, then say what we are doing about it.

    Is this not just a workflow?

    Partly, and on purpose. It has the building blocks of an agent; its autonomy grows one step at a time as each step passes its test.

    Show me a skill or the build plan.

    They are used in the week-3 pipeline (see status.md, tasks 2.1 and 6.1). The files themselves are not in this repo yet; that is an open point.

    Does the tool save time?

    We do not know yet. There is one logged run of 43 minutes in timelog.csv; that is not a baseline yet.

    BAS World is not an SME.

    It is a frontier case: it shows what stays hard even with budget and IT. Interview questions 12 and 13 ask directly what a small trader can copy.

    Why is the interview kept outside the tool?

    Privacy by design. The recording and transcripts never go to an AI model; a person enters the statements into claims_en.md by hand (Blueprint section 4).
