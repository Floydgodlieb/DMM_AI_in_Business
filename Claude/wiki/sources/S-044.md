# S-044 · World Trade Report 2025: Making trade and AI work together to the benefit of all (Executive summary)

## What it says

- In a 2025 WTO and International Chamber of Commerce survey, nearly 90 per cent of firms already using AI report tangible benefits in trade-related activities and 56 per cent say it improved their ability to manage trade risks; these are firms reporting on themselves, worldwide [W4-075].
- The WTO says AI tools already improve visibility in supply chains, automate customs clearance, reduce language barriers and help small firms handle complex rules [W4-076].
- Scenario projections suggest AI could raise global trade by 34 to 37 per cent by 2040 [W4-077] and global GDP by 12 to 13 per cent [W4-078]; these are worldwide scenarios, not forecasts for one sector.
