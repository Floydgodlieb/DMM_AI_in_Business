# CBS (Statistics Netherlands)

The Dutch national statistics office. Its pages are rated at the top of the credibility scale because they are official statistics on the issuer's own domain.

## What the library has from CBS

- AI use by Dutch firms with 10+ staff in 2024 [W2-001], by size [W2-002], with small firms under-represented [W2-003] and trade the largest sector among users [W2-004]. See [[ai-adoption-rates]].
- 2025 figures (provisional): one in six businesses uses AI [W4-155]; firms with 50 to 250 staff up to 45 percent [W4-156]; privacy and legal worries as reasons not to use [W4-159]. See [[ai-adoption-rates]], [[knowledge-gap]].
- A survey of adults (not firms): 43 percent of people in paid work use AI at work [W4-139]; opinions on jobs and productivity [W4-140] [W4-141]. See [[ai-and-jobs]].
- Why micro firms decide against AI [W4-023] [W4-024], and how few firms employ ICT specialists [W4-025]. See [[knowledge-gap]].
- [[S-031]] (the CBS AI-monitor) is in the library without claims yet.
