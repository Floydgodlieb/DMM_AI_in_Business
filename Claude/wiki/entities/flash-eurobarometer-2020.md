# Flash Eurobarometer survey (spring 2020)

Two of the library's peer-reviewed studies rest on the same EU business survey. Its timing limits what they can say about today's AI tools.

## What to know

- Fielded 19 February to 5 May 2020, with 16,365 firms [W4-059]; one study uses 12,108 SMEs from it [W4-047].
- It predates generative AI tools [W4-050].
- It reports no Netherlands-only result [W4-060].

Any finding built on it ([[learning-inside-the-firm]], [[knowledge-gap]], [[ai-adoption-rates]]) describes AI before ChatGPT-style tools.
