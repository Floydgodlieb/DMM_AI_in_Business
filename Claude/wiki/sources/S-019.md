# S-019 · AI Literacy - Questions & Answers

## What it says

- Any business that provides or uses AI systems must take measures to build AI literacy among the people who run or use them [W4-003].
- The Commission and Member States must support businesses, in particular SMEs, in meeting their duties [W4-004].
- The duty does not require measuring employees' AI knowledge [W4-005].
- It has applied since 2 February 2025; supervision and enforcement apply from 3 August 2026 [W4-006].
- National market surveillance authorities enforce it, not the AI Office [W4-007].
