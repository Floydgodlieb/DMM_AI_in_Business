# S-061 · The Rapid Adoption of Generative AI (Bick, Blandin and Deming; NBER Working Paper 32966, September 2024, revised February 2025)

## What it says

- An NBER working paper finds that as of late 2024 nearly 40 percent of the US population aged 18-64 uses generative AI; this is a population survey, not a firm survey [W4-132].
- 23 percent of employed respondents had used generative AI for work in the previous week, and 9 percent used it every work day [W4-133].
- Between 1 and 5 percent of all work hours are assisted by generative AI, and respondents report time savings equal to 1.4 percent of total work hours [W4-134].
- The data come from the Real-Time Population Survey, a national online survey of US adults aged 18-64 that has run since 2020 [W4-135].
