# S-045 · EU Customs Reform

## What it says

- On 16 September 2026 the European Parliament and the Council adopted what the Commission calls the most comprehensive reform of the EU Customs Union since 1968; the source is the Commission's policy page, not the legal text [W4-079].
- A new EU Customs Authority will manage the EU Customs Data Hub, the single digital interface for all customs operations in the EU [W4-081].
- Using the data hub to record imports and exports becomes mandatory for e-commerce on 1 July 2028 and for all traders from 1 March 2034 [W4-080].
- Reliable "Trust & Check" traders get new facilitations, including the possibility to self-release goods on behalf of customs [W4-082].
- Nothing in the claims says AI is part of the reform; it bears on the export-document step as a change in how data is filed.
