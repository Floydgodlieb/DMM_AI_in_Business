# Geopolitics of AI

The US leads on investment, China pushes adoption by plan, and Europe worries about competitiveness; for a small firm, this shows up as dependence on foreign providers.

## The picture

- The US commits 23 times more private AI investment than China [W2-015], though state funds likely understate China's spend [W2-016].
- China targets more than 70% penetration of AI agents and intelligent terminals in key sectors by 2027 [W4-002], an edge MERICS says could reach industrial automation [W2-018].
- The Draghi report (9 September 2024) [W4-001] finds Europe's prosperity under pressure from slowing productivity and global competition [W2-012].
- EU firms match US firms on generative AI use [W4-036].
- Outside the US and China, firms live in managed dependence on foreign AI [W2-019].

## Contradictions and tensions

> [!contradiction] W2-017 vs W4-002: same target, second-hand and first-hand
> MERICS reports the 70% target [W2-017]; the State Council states it [W4-002]. They agree; cite the primary where possible.
