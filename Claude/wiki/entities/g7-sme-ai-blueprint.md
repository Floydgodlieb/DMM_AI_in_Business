# G7 SME AI Adoption Blueprint

A programme of the G7 industry, digital and technology ministers (December 2025). The ministers' statement and the OECD discussion paper prepared for the Canadian G7 presidency agree on the main finding. The Netherlands is not a G7 member [W4-144].

## What the library has

- SME adoption lags large enterprises in all G7 countries [W4-143] [W4-178].
- Sector gaps: construction, hospitality and transportation remain in the single digits [W4-145]; low adoption in manufacturing, transport, construction and agriculture is put down to legacy systems, limited digital maturity and lack of internal AI expertise [W4-146].
- OECD-wide, large firms' use is more than three times small firms' use [W4-179].
