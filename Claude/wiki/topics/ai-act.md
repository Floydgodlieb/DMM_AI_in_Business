# The AI Act

The AI Act sets duties for both providers and users (deployers) of AI systems [W4-011]; for a trader, the literacy duty applies to any AI use, and heavier duties apply only to high-risk systems.

## What the library covers

- **Every user:** the AI-literacy duty, see [[ai-literacy-duty]] [W4-003]. The Article 4 text itself says companies must take measures to build AI literacy among staff and others using AI on their behalf [W4-091], without guaranteeing any level for any individual [W4-092].
- **Users of high-risk AI (Article 26):** use the system according to the provider's instructions [W2-009] [W4-093], assign trained human oversight [W4-094], keep the automatically generated logs for at least six months [W4-095], and tell people when they are subject to it [W2-010].
- **Fines (Article 99):** banned practices can bring fines up to EUR 35 000 000 or 7 % of worldwide turnover, whichever is higher [W4-096]; for SMEs the lower of the amount and percentage applies [W4-097]. No claim gives the fine for breaching the literacy duty.
- **Support:** Member States must help SMEs meet their duties [W4-004].

## Contradictions and tensions

> [!contradiction] W2-009 vs W4-093: same duty, now from the primary text
> The Article 26 duty to follow the instructions for use appears first via a mirror of the regulation [W2-009], now via the Commission's AI Act Service Desk text [W4-093]. They agree; cite the Service Desk text.

## Gaps

- No claim says which trader activities, if any, count as high-risk.
- The Service Desk text is the Commission's rendering of the regulation, not EUR-Lex; all claims await a person check.
- [[S-024]] (KVK on entrepreneurs' AI Act awareness) has no claim yet.
