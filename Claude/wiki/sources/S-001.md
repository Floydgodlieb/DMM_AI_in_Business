# S-001 · Gebruik kunstmatige intelligentie (AI) door bedrijven neemt toe

## What it says

- In 2024, 22.7% of Dutch firms with 10 or more staff used at least one AI technology, almost 9 points more than in 2023 [W2-001].
- Use rises with size: highest at 500+ staff (59.2%), lowest at 10-19 staff (17.8%) [W2-002].
