# S-059 · Generative AI at Work (Brynjolfsson, Li and Raymond; NBER Working Paper 31161, April 2023, revised November 2023; later published in the Quarterly Journal of Economics)

## What it says

- An NBER working paper studies the staggered introduction of a generative AI chat assistant using data from 5,179 customer support agents [W4-125].
- Access raised productivity (issues resolved per hour) by 14% on average, including 34% for novice and low-skilled workers, but with minimal impact on experienced and highly skilled workers [W4-126].
- The agents worked for one Fortune 500 software firm, so the result comes from one large firm, not from small traders [W4-127].
