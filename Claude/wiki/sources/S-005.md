# S-005 · "Article 26: Obligations of Deployers of High-Risk AI Systems (EU AI Act, Regulation (EU) 2024/1689)"

## What it says

- A business deploying a high-risk AI system must use it according to the provider's instructions, with fitting technical and organisational measures [W2-009].
- It must tell people when they are subject to the system's use [W2-010].
- This is a mirror of the regulation, not EUR-Lex itself; both claims await a person check.
