# S-055 · AI and the euro area economy (keynote speech by Philip R. Lane, ECB-SAFE-RCEA conference, Frankfurt, 23 March 2026)

## What it says

- An ECB board member cites a 2024 IMF study estimating that almost 40 per cent of global employment is exposed to AI, rising to around 60 per cent in advanced economies; exposure is not the same as jobs lost [W4-110].
- The ECB's Consumer Expectations Survey shows the share of euro area employees adopting AI rose from 26 per cent in 2024 to 40 per cent in 2025 [W4-111].
- The speech concludes there is currently little evidence of a substantial effect of AI on employment in the euro area [W4-112].
- Faster adoption could yield productivity gains of 0.3-0.4 percentage points per year over the coming decade, depending on how much of GDP is exposed; this is a scenario, not a measured result [W4-113].
