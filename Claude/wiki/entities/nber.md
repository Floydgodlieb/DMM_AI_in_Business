# NBER (National Bureau of Economic Research)

A US economics research network. Everything from it in the library is a working paper; several were later published in journals (S-043 in Management Science, S-054 in Economic Policy, S-059 in the Quarterly Journal of Economics). Read each figure as a working-paper result unless the published version is the one cited.

## What the library has from NBER

- Machine translation and trade on eBay, 2018 [W4-071] [W4-074]. See [[ai-in-trade-and-customs]].
- Macro estimate of AI: modest [W4-107]. See [[ai-and-the-economy]].
- Customer-support productivity [W4-126]. See [[genai-productivity-at-work]].
- Danish labour market effects [W4-129] and US firm-level labour effects [W4-136]. See [[ai-and-jobs]].
- US adoption survey [W4-132]. See [[ai-adoption-rates]].

## Tension to keep in view

Taken together, NBER papers report both large task-level gains [W4-126] and small economy-wide or labour-market effects [W4-107] [W4-129]; see the flagged contradictions on [[genai-productivity-at-work]].
