# S-042 · The new normal: The status quo of AI adoption in SMEs (Julia Schwaeke, Anna Peters, Dominik K. Kanbach, Sascha Kraus & Paul Jones; Journal of Small Business Management, 2025, 63(3), 1297-1331)

## What it says

- Complex AI may not be feasible for small firms because of cost and complexity (Moeuf et al.) [W4-062].
- Poor expertise, skills and management styles hold small firms back (Liu) [W4-063]; so does a lack of AI specialists (Lemos et al.) [W4-064]; and they fear the costs (Dorr et al.) [W4-065].
- Education and practical training give staff confidence (Hamdan et al.) [W4-066].
- Small firms partner with outside organisations (Qu et al.) [W4-067] and use networks as sources of innovation (Hermawati and Gunawan) [W4-068].
- Management commitment can offset limited resources (Rojas-Cordova et al.) [W4-069]; the advice is a learning culture, workforce investment, open communication and adaptable leadership [W4-070].
