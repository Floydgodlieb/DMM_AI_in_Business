# Where to get AI knowledge from outside

Outside help exists and SMEs ask for it, but the evidence says it works best alongside the firm's own learning, and its effects can fade.

## What SMEs ask for

- Practical knowledge, information, guidance and financial support [W4-029].
- The SER advises practical support for SMEs [W4-030] and a network where firms share experience [W4-031].

## Routes the library names

- **European Digital Innovation Hubs** ([[edih]]): EDIH Zuid-Nederland serves southern manufacturing SMEs [W4-018], so a trader may not qualify [W4-019]; the Northwest hub covers other regions and sectors [W4-012]. EU-wide, the hubs ran over 5 000 events and 18 000 services [W4-033].
- **Digiwerkplaats mkb** ([[digiwerkplaats-mkb]]): Avans helps owners in West- and Noordoost-Brabant with digitalisation questions [W4-017].
- **Your supplier:** ask what training implementation needs [W4-010].
- **Partners and networks:** small firms partner with outside organisations [W4-067] and use networks as sources of innovation [W4-068].

## What the evidence says it achieves

- The hubs report that 90% of users improved their digital maturity, by their own monitoring [W4-034].
- A UK trial of a voucher for outside expert advice found the knowledge gain fades within two years [W4-042] and does not close gaps in the long run [W4-046].
- In 2020 data, outside support mattered least for AI adoption, after the firm's own digital and innovation capabilities [W4-052].

## Contradictions and tensions

> [!contradiction] W4-034 vs W4-046: hubs report lasting improvement; an independent trial of bought-in advice finds the gain fades
> The hub figure is self-reported by the network [W4-034]; the trial is a randomised study, but of a UK voucher, not AI and not hubs [W4-041]. Not the same intervention, so both can hold.

> [!contradiction] W4-029 vs W4-048: SMEs ask for outside guidance; the evidence weighs own capabilities more
> Read together: outside help is wanted and useful as a start, but adoption depends on building capability inside ([[learning-inside-the-firm]]).
