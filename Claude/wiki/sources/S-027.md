# S-027 · European Digital Innovation Hub (EDIH) Zuid-Nederland van start!

## What it says

- Manufacturing SMEs from Zeeland, Noord-Brabant and Limburg can turn to EDIH Zuid-Nederland for digitalisation help [W4-018].
- It targets manufacturing and its suppliers, so a used-vehicle trader may not qualify [W4-019].
