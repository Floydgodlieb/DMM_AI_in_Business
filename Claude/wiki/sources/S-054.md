# S-054 · The Simple Macroeconomics of AI (NBER Working Paper 32487, May 2024; published in Economic Policy, 2025)

## What it says

- An NBER working paper by Daron Acemoglu (later published in Economic Policy) estimates the macroeconomic effects of AI as nontrivial but modest: no more than a 0.66% increase in total factor productivity over 10 years [W4-107].
- It argues the early evidence comes from easy-to-learn tasks, so gains over the next 10 years are likely even smaller, below 0.53% [W4-108].
- It predicts that AI will widen the gap between income from capital and income from labor [W4-109].
