# S-050 · Article 99: Penalties (Regulation (EU) 2024/1689), AI Act Service Desk

## What it says

- Article 99: breaking the ban on the AI practices listed in Article 5 can bring fines of up to EUR 35 000 000 or, for a company, up to 7 % of total worldwide annual turnover of the preceding year, whichever is higher [W4-096].
- For SMEs, including start-ups, each fine is capped at the percentage or amount set in the relevant paragraphs, whichever is lower [W4-097].
- The claims cover only the top fine for banned practices and the SME rule; no claim gives the fine for breaching the literacy duty.
