# S-073 · Gen-AI: Artificial Intelligence and the Future of Work (IMF Staff Discussion Note SDN/2024/001)

## What it says

- Almost 40 percent of global employment is exposed to AI, with advanced economies at greater risk but also better placed to exploit its benefits; this measures exposure of occupations, not realised productivity [W4-183].
- In advanced economies about 60 percent of jobs are exposed, because cognitive-task jobs are common; exposure is not jobs lost [W4-184].
- Of the exposed jobs, about half may be negatively affected by AI, while the rest could benefit from higher productivity [W4-185].
- Overall exposure is 40 percent in emerging market economies and 26 percent in low-income countries [W4-186].
