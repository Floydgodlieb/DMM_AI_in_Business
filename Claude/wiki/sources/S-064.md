# S-064 · G7 Industry, Digital and Technology Ministerial Statement on the SME AI Adoption Blueprint (Background, G7 SME AI Adoption Blueprint, 9 December 2025)

## What it says

- Although more SMEs are adopting AI, in all G7 countries their adoption rate lags that of large enterprises [W4-143].
- Companies with 250+ employees adopt more; in Germany, France and Italy large companies adopt AI at 48.2%, 32.7% and 32.5%; the Netherlands is not a G7 country [W4-144].
- ICT and professional services are far above average, while construction, hospitality and transportation remain in the single digits [W4-145].
- Manufacturing, transportation, construction and agriculture adopt less because of legacy systems, limited digital maturity and a lack of internal AI expertise [W4-146].
