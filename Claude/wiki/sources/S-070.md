# S-070 · The Effects of Generative AI on High-Skilled Work: Evidence from Three Field Experiments with Software Developers (draft, February 2025)

## What it says

- Label: an author draft, not a journal version; two authors are Microsoft employees and the tool studied is a Microsoft/GitHub product [W4-167].
- Combining three randomized experiments (Microsoft, Accenture, an anonymous Fortune 100 company) and 4,867 developers shows a 26.08% increase (SE: 10.3%) in completed tasks among developers using the tool; the subjects work at large firms, not trading SMEs [W4-168].
- Less experienced developers had higher adoption rates and greater productivity gains [W4-169].
- The authors say the experiments were implemented rather ad hoc, driven by business considerations rather than research goals [W4-170].
