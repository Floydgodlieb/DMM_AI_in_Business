## Context

Why this decision is on the table now — the forces at play, what is currently
undecided, and what breaks if it stays undecided. Written so that someone
picking this up next year understands the situation without having been in the
room.

## Options considered

Each realistic option with what it costs. An option nobody would seriously pick
does not belong here; an option that was rejected for a non-obvious reason does.

## Decision

The decision in one sentence, then the reasoning. While the status is
`Proposed`, this is the proposal on the table — it still states an outcome, so
that the record forces a yes or no rather than another round of deliberation.

## Consequences

What becomes true because of this — including what it makes harder, more
expensive, or impossible. A record that lists only benefits has not been thought
through.
