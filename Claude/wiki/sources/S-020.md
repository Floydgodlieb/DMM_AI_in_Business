# S-020 · Personeel en gebruikers zijn voldoende AI-geletterd

## What it says

- The framework requires staff and users to be sufficiently AI-literate; it is written for government bodies, but the duty comes from the AI Act [W4-008].
- It advises awareness and enough training on the risks and opportunities of AI [W4-009].
- It advises asking the supplier what training and support implementation needs [W4-010].
