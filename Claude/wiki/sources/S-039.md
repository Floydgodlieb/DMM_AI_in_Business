# S-039 · Subsidized R&D collaboration: The causal effect of innovation vouchers on innovation outcomes

## What it says

- The study tested a UK voucher of up to 5,000 GBP that lets small firms buy outside expert advice on an innovation project; it is not about AI [W4-041].
- The voucher let firms hire experts they had not worked with, to gain new knowledge [W4-043], mostly for small projects rather than breakthroughs [W4-044].
- The firms were very small: on average 7 staff, mostly in services [W4-045].
- Many effects had gone two years later [W4-042]; the voucher closed a knowledge gap for one project but not in the long run [W4-046].
