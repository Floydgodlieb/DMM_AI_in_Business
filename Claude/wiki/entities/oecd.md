# OECD

An intergovernmental research body. Two papers in the library: a survey of SMEs in seven countries (not the Netherlands) and a discussion paper prepared for the Canadian G7 presidency.

## What the library has from the OECD

- Survey (self-reports by SMEs): generative AI in 31% of SMEs [W4-173]; 65% say it helped employee performance [W4-174]; 83% say no effect on staff need [W4-175]. The Netherlands is not covered [W4-171].
- Barriers: unsuitability, legal worries, data worries and skills [W4-176]; few firms train staff or set guidelines [W4-177]. See [[knowledge-gap]], [[ai-literacy-duty]].
- Discussion paper: large firms' AI use is more than three times that of small firms [W4-179]; use rose from 5.6% to 14% in 2020 to 2024 [W4-180]. See [[ai-adoption-rates]], [[g7-sme-ai-blueprint]].
- Risks SMEs report: accuracy, harmful content, legal uncertainty [W4-182]. See [[ai-reliability-and-hallucination]].
