excerpt: |
  (publication date) 22 May 2025

  (the gap) "the laws of agency and vicarious liability require there first to be a human
  agent...before their employer...can be held responsible" — with a truly autonomous AI agent,
  "there may be no human 'employee' acting at the moment of harm."

  (EU product liability) the revised EU Product Liability Directive "extends traditional
  product-liability rules to cover software and AI," so developers can face strict liability
  for harm the AI causes.

  (AI Act's own silence) the AI Act "lays down a comprehensive framework" but remains "silent"
  on who is responsible after harm occurs — it is built to prevent harm, not to assign blame
  once it has happened.
