# S-053 · Large Legal Fictions: Profiling Legal Hallucinations in Large Language Models (Dahl, Magesh, Suzgun, Ho; Journal of Legal Analysis, 2024)

## What it says

- The authors of this peer-reviewed study say they present the first systematic evidence of legal hallucinations in public-facing language models; it covers legal questions, so the link to business text is by analogy [W4-104].
- Asked a direct, verifiable question about a randomly selected US federal court case, the models tested made things up between 58% of the time (ChatGPT 4) and 88% of the time (Llama 2) [W4-105].
- The authors caution against fast, unsupervised use of popular models for legal tasks [W4-106].
