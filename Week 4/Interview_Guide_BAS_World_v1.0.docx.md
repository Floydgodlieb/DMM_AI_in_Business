**BAS WORLD HANDBOOK PROJECT**

# **Interview Guide: AI in the sales chain at BAS World**

About 35 minutes. Version 1.0, 26 September 2026\.

| Interviewee (job role) |  | Date |  |
| :---- | :---- | :---- | :---- |
| **Interviewer and note-taker** |  | **Consent signed before the visit** | yes / no |

**Goal.** Learn how far AI reaches into BAS World’s chain from lead to export document (I1), where staff draw the line on automation that touches the customer (I3), and what the next automated step would take (I2). Then ask what a trader with 10 to 50 staff and no IT person could realistically copy.

**Who gets which questions.** Sales or customer service: all questions except 11\. Export documentation: all questions. The person who approves tooling spend (about 10 minutes): questions 8, 9, 12 and 13\.

**While interviewing.** Ask for one recent example and a rough number every time. For each answer, note the chain step (lead, quotation, contract, payment, export document) and whether the use saves staff time or touches the customer. Do not name laws or tools before the interviewee does. Questions 4 and 7 are the same question, asked before and after the examples.

| Opening, read aloud “Thank you for your time. We are four HAN students writing a practical handbook for small traders in used trucks, trailers and machinery, with BAS World as our case. We want to understand what actually happens in your work, not what we can find online. By AI we mean any tool that writes, translates, answers or decides for you, including chatbots and automatic translation. Some questions touch on rules, but we are not checking compliance. With your permission we record this talk. The recording stays on one team laptop, never goes into an AI tool, and is deleted after the course. We use your job role, not your name. You can skip any question and check every quote before we publish. We will send you a short summary within \[X\] working days. May we start the recording?” |
| :---- |

### **Part 1\. The sales chain today (about 10 minutes)**

| No. | Question and follow-up questions | Feeds | Notes |
| :---- | :---- | :---- | :---- |
| 1 | **What is your role, and which steps from first lead to export document do you work on yourself?** Which of your tasks are the same every time? | I1 |  |
| 2 | **Take one used truck, trailer or machine you recently sold to a buyer outside the EU. Walk us through it from first lead to export document. At which steps did AI play a part, and at which steps none?** What started each AI step, what did the tool produce, and what did a person do next? Which step still costs the most manual work or delay, and why has AI not reached it? | I1, E1, criterion 1 |  |
| 3 | **If that AI tool disappeared tomorrow, how would the same task be done?** How many people and how much time, then and now? How often is it used per week, and which errors or rework remain? | I1, criterion 4 |  |

### **Part 2\. Trust and the boundary (about 9 minutes)**

| No. | Question and follow-up questions | Feeds | Notes |
| :---- | :---- | :---- | :---- |
| 4 | **Is there any task where AI may act on BAS World’s behalf without a person approving it first, such as sending a message, changing a price or filling in a document?** Where do you draw the line between AI that may act and AI that may only advise? Write this answer down word for word. You ask it again in question 7\. | I3 |  |
| 5 | **Which of your AI uses only save staff time, and which ones does a customer actually see or receive?** Can you give one example of each? Were the two kinds treated differently in testing, approval or responsibility? | I3, criterion 2 |  |
| 6 | **Before AI-generated work reaches a customer or an authority, what checks happen, and who is responsible?** Can you give a recent example of an AI output you accepted, changed or rejected, and why? Can you describe a time when a check caught a problem? | I3, week 3 |  |
| 7 | **Now that we have talked through these examples: would you still draw the line where you did at the start? Why or why not?** Note whether the answer moved, and in which direction. | I3 (before and after) |  |

### **Part 3\. What the next step would take (about 5 minutes)**

| No. | Question and follow-up questions | Feeds | Notes |
| :---- | :---- | :---- | :---- |
| 8 | **Which recurring task would you hand to AI next, and what would that take?** What is the bottleneck now, and how often does it occur? Roughly what would it cost, which people would it need, and who would own it? What result would count as an improvement? | I2, criterion 5 |  |
| 9 | **Have you tried AI for something and then stopped? Why?** Who decided, and what had it cost by then? | I2 |  |

### **Part 4\. Data and rules (about 4 minutes)**

| No. | Question and follow-up questions | Feeds | Notes |
| :---- | :---- | :---- | :---- |
| 10 | **Which information may never go into a public AI tool, and how do staff know where that line is?** Is there a written rule, a list of approved tools, or training? Do you know in which country the tools you use process your data? | E2, E3, criterion 3 |  |
| 11 | **Only for export documentation staff. Does AI play any part in sanctions screening or export paperwork, or has it been kept out on purpose?** Who stays responsible if a tool misses an error? Which rule tells you what is required? | E3, criterion 3 |  |

### **Part 5\. Lessons for a small trader (about 6 minutes)**

| No. | Question and follow-up questions | Feeds | Notes |
| :---- | :---- | :---- | :---- |
| 12 | **BAS World has budget and its own IT staff. Which AI problem is still hard for you despite that?** Which of your solutions could a trader with 10 to 50 staff and no IT person not copy, and what could they do instead? | Main question, case or sector |  |
| 13 | **A trader with 10 to 50 staff and no IT person wants to run a 30-day AI pilot. What should they test first?** Buy a ready-made tool, make one person responsible for one process, or build something themselves? Who should own it, what would it roughly cost per month, and what should they check after three months? What should they definitely not do? | Criteria 5 and 6 |  |
