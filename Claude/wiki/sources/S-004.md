# S-004 · Sanctions adopted following Russia's military aggression against Ukraine

## What it says

- The EU bans exports to Russia of dual-use goods and advanced technology, including semiconductors and software [W2-006].
- It also restricts exports of transport equipment and goods for aviation, space and maritime navigation [W2-007].
- The legal basis is Council Regulation (EU) No 833/2014 [W2-008].
