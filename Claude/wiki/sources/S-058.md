# S-058 · Generative AI and jobs: A 2025 update (ILO research brief, 20 May 2025; based on ILO Working Paper 140)

## What it says

- One in four workers worldwide are in an occupation with some exposure to generative AI, but because human input is still needed, most jobs will be transformed rather than made redundant; this is global, not Europe-specific [W4-122].
- The updated mean automation score is 0.29 in 2025, slightly below 0.30 in 2023, with much less variability [W4-123].
- The 2025 update uses a refined method drawing on human and AI insight, assessed at the 6-digit occupational level and covering nearly 30,000 tasks [W4-124].
