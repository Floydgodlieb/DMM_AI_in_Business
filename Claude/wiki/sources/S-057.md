# S-057 · 20% of EU enterprises use AI technologies (Eurostat news article, 11 December 2025)

## What it says

- In 2025, 20.0% of EU enterprises with 10 or more employees used AI technologies, up 6.5 percentage points from 13.5% in 2024 [W4-118].
- The highest shares were in Denmark (42.0%), Finland (37.8%) and Sweden (35.0%) and the lowest in Romania (5.2%), Poland (8.4%) and Bulgaria (8.5%); the Netherlands is not in this extract [W4-119].
- The most common use was to analyse written language (11.8%) [W4-120].
- The data come from the 2025 EU survey on ICT usage and e-commerce and cover enterprises with at least 10 employees or self-employed persons, so smaller firms are not included [W4-121].
