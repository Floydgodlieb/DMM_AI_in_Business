## Rephrasings used to research the original question (not new questions — the same one, reworded, per context.md §6's question-diversity rule)

| Part | Neutral phrasing | Differently-angled phrasing | Did the angle change what surfaced? |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1. What leaves the building | "Where does our data actually go when we use an AI tool?" | "What happens to our data if we stop trusting the AI tool's owner?" | Some — the second phrasing is what led to checking the Digital Omnibus delay and the SME-proportionality change, not just vendor hosting pages. |
| 2. Act or only advise | "What are the benefits of letting AI agents act for a business?" | "Who pays when the AI agent gets it wrong?" | Yes, substantially — see searchlog.md. The first phrasing returned governance-framework and productivity content with no concrete failure case. The second returned a named, decided legal case (Moffatt v. Air Canada) and the actual liability-cap mechanics a vendor contract uses to limit its own exposure. An owner who only asks the first version of this question would not find the second version's answer. |
| 3. Staff using AI without saying so | "employees using AI tools without company approval" (English) | "werknemers gebruiken AI zonder toestemming werkgever" (Dutch) | Yes — different language surfaced different primary evidence, not just a translation of the same result: English surfaced an international breach-cost study (IBM/Ponemon); Dutch surfaced a Netherlands-specific adoption survey (Awareways). Both are kept, each labelled for its own scope. |
